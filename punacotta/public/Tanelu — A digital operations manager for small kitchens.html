01Onboarding

What else could it be, other than simple?

Tanelu was built in response to restaurant management solutions that demand implementation engineers, heaps of outdated documentation, scheduled visits and training workshops. Fast-forward a few weeks and you're filing a ticket with customer support. Fast-forward again and there's no one left to onboard new team members.

Business interruptions? Say that again. You have no time, and we genuinely value that. We don't allocate teams to rescue people lost in navigation or documentation — but we do want to hear back from you.

02Minimum overhead

Minimum overhead. No, really.

You don't wake up wanting dashboards. What you want is less micromanagement. Tanelu doesn't pretend to be an all-in-one solution for hotels, chain stores and space platforms.

It was built for Noah at the pastry shop downstairs, and for Zoey making the best long-fermented cheeses in the neighborhood. They dream big — but that doesn't mean they dream of an ERP. They want a digital operations manager that respects their attention span. We think that holds true for a business of any size.

04Single source of truth

One screen. Because it's just natural.

Customers would rather order pizza and drinks in one place and pay once, without switching between apps. First impressions matter — and so does the second, third and hundredth order.

The same idea drives Tanelu. A kitchen receives orders from many sources: by phone, online, from Yandex or Glovo. An “all-in-one leading industry solution” usually means a POS app, a kitchen screen, a third-party waiter app and licensed supplier connectors — a patchwork of tools with wildly inconsistent interfaces.

That's where Tanelu perfects the unified interface. You want one arrivals-and-departures screen, because it's just natural. With everything on a single screen, your team spends less time switching tabs, learning interfaces, or waiting for someone to troubleshoot yet another integration.
